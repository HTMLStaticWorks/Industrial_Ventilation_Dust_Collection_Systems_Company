About AirForge

Engineering Cleaner Industrial Air

For over 15 years, AirForge has designed, installed, and maintained ventilation and dust collection systems that keep facilities safe, compliant, and running.

Who We Are

A Turnkey Air Quality Partner

AirForge Industrial Systems is an engineering-led company specializing in dust collection, fume extraction, and industrial ventilation. We work with manufacturers, foundries, fabricators, and processing plants to solve airborne contaminant problems at the source.

Our in-house engineers handle every phase — facility audits, CFD airflow modeling, system design, fabrication, installation, and long-term maintenance — so you have one accountable team from first survey to final sign-off.

Mission & Values

Company Milestones
