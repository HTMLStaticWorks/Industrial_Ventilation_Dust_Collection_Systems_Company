Heavy Metal Fabrication
Case Study

Ambient Weld Fume Control

Deployed a central push-pull ventilation system across a 40,000 sq ft fabrication floor, replacing inefficient localized fans.

The Challenge

Our client, a leading heavy metal fabrication facility spanning 40,000 square feet, was struggling with high concentrations of ambient weld fumes. Localized extraction arms were proving inefficient due to the sheer size of the components being welded. The resulting air quality was poor, posing health risks to operators and leading to potential OSHA compliance issues.

The Solution

AirForge engineering team conducted a comprehensive computational fluid dynamics (CFD) analysis to design a state-of-the-art central push-pull ventilation system. The solution involved:

  • Installing a series of high-velocity push nozzles along one side of the facility.
  • Implementing opposing pull grilles connected to a high-capacity HEPA filtration unit.
  • Creating a continuous directional airflow pattern that captured fumes before they reached the operators' breathing zones.
  • Integrating smart sensors to adjust fan speeds based on real-time air quality metrics, optimizing energy usage.

The Results

The implementation of the central push-pull ventilation system dramatically transformed the facility's environment. The air quality improvements were immediate and measurable, ensuring a safer workspace and full compliance with all regulatory standards.
