Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries about our engineering and installation process.

How long does a typical installation take?

Most of our custom extraction systems are installed over a weekend or during scheduled plant downtime to minimize operational impact. Complex facility-wide deployments may be done in phases.

Do you guarantee OSHA/NFPA compliance?

Yes. Every system we design is built specifically to meet or exceed current OSHA and NFPA standards for your particular industry and dust/fume type.

Do you service equipment built by other manufacturers?

Yes, our maintenance and audit teams can evaluate, service, and upgrade existing extraction systems regardless of the original manufacturer.
