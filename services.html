Engineering

System Design & Engineering

Every facility has unique airflow, structural, and hazard constraints. We do not sell off-the-shelf equipment; we design engineered solutions. Our process begins with a comprehensive facility audit, examining particulate types, generation rates, and existing HVAC loads.

  • CFD Airflow Modeling: Visualizing extraction efficiency before installation.
  • Compliance Auditing: Ensuring designs meet OSHA, EPA, and NFPA standards.
  • Custom Duct Engineering: Minimizing static pressure loss and optimizing conveying velocities.

Upkeep

Filter Maintenance Programs

An extraction system is only as effective as its filtration media. Neglected filters lead to reduced CFM, motor strain, and unsafe ambient conditions. AirForge offers structured maintenance contracts to ensure your systems remain operational and compliant year-round.

Tier 1: Essential Quarterly

Basic inspection, pulse-jet valve testing, and primary media replacement.

Tier 2: Comprehensive Monthly

Full system diagnostic, airflow telemetry review, motor bearing lubrication, and priority emergency response.
